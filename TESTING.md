# Manual playtest checklist

Automated checks do not replace this graphical review.

- Title text fits and explains the goal.
- Enter/Space starts a fresh run; five beans and four hazards are visible.
- WASD/arrows move smoothly; diagonals are not faster.
- Pickups increment the counter, play a note, and create particles.
- Hazard hits remove one life, flash the dog, and briefly protect it.
- P/Escape and focus loss pause; resume does not jump ahead.
- Zero lives or timer expiry shows the end screen.
- Twenty beans shows the winning screen.
- R and Enter/Space restart without stale particles or counters.
- M toggles audio; resizing keeps the arena readable.
- Best score persists after relaunch.
